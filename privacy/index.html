Homepace privacy policy

Effective: October 10, 2026

Homepace is a home-chores app for Android. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your chores stay on your phone, and I never receive them.

What the app stores

The chores you add, their schedules, your completion history, and your settings are saved in a database on your phone. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server. The developer cannot see your data.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, send or sell personal information.
  • It has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • It does not connect to the internet to work.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows a daily summary of your chores. These notifications are created on your phone. No notification service or server is involved. You can turn them off in the app's settings or in Android's system settings.

Sharing

The app can turn your recap into an image and open Android's share sheet. That happens only when you tap share, and you choose where the image goes. The app does not share anything on its own.

Backups

Android may back up the app's data to your own Google account as part of Android's standard device backup, and restore it when you set up a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account's settings and terms. You can turn it off in your phone's system settings. The developer has no access to it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes the data from your phone. If you used Android backup, you can delete that copy from your Google account's backup settings.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
